La Necar Logistics: Building a clearer brand and opening a new market

Brand strategy, market expansion, email marketing, website copy, community outreach | La Necar Logistics

The challenge

La Necar was evolving its disability transportation services for adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities and expanding from New Jersey into Pennsylvania, New York and Connecticut. It needed a clearer way to communicate the brand value, while reaching relevant organizations and care teams. Pennsylvania was a new market, with no established contact list or prior presence.

What shaped my thinking

I saw two connected questions: what do the people arranging and using these rides actually need, and what can the business deliver through its existing processes and operations?

The Idea

Build the brand strategy around the needs of the people it serves and the core strengths of the brand operations. Then use that clearer message to guide targeted outreach, create new referral routes and support the Pennsylvania expansion with relevant email contacts for marketing campaigns

How I executed it

  • Worked with Operations, IT, Fleet, Customer Service and Behavior teams to understand how transportation was delivered, what was often prioritized and develop customer personas from available data.
  • Translated those findings into brand messaging, voice and identity used across the company's marketing, and drafted website copy with a clearer customer-facing value propositions.
  • Researched audience conversations in online communities, including Facebook groups and Reddit, to inform campaign themes and language.
  • For the Pennsylvania expansion, used AI to help build an automated contact-collection tool for a 9,000-contact disability-services directory. The tool filtered records by county, organization and available contact details, then onboarded 955 relevant contacts to the CRM and expanded the list beyond 1,000.
  • Planned and sent three targeted B2B email campaigns a week to relevant contact segments, then monitored campaign performance and enquiries.
  • Developed a healthcare-provider outreach concept and prototyped Ride Prescription Cards to help providers direct patients to medical transportation. Also conducted an external community survey informing an advocacy marketing strategy.
  • Tracked 14 transportation requests from coverage areas in Pennsylvania alone between July and October 2026, each representing an estimated 5–30 potential riders. When I identified delays in fulfilling requests, I led a team in reporting the service and reputation impact to leadership, which prompted immediate driver assignments to pending requests within coverage areas.

What changed

The work gave La Necar a more consistent brand foundation, a targeted Pennsylvania contact base and new outreach routes through healthcare providers and community advocacy. Email campaigns generated qualified enquiries and transportation requests in a market where the company previously had no presence. The enquiry tracking also helped bring operational delays to leadership's attention.

Four Corners Community Services: Turning campaign goals into coordinated outreach

Campaign development, partnership research, proposal writing, team coordination | Four Corners Community Services

The challenge

The work involved two different goals: attracting support for a Thanksgiving donation campaign and building relationships with businesses around a supported-employment initiative.

What shaped my thinking

Each goal needed a clear reason for an external organization to get involved. The donation campaign needed its message and materials ready in time for the seasonal moment. The employment initiative needed a considered list of potential business partners and a proposal that could explain the opportunity.

The strategy

Match the outreach to the decision being asked of each audience: make the donation campaign timely and clear, and make the partnership approach relevant to the businesses being approached.

How I executed it

  • Led the creative development of the Thanksgiving donation campaign and coordinated marketing deliverables ahead of the deadline.
  • Researched New Jersey businesses for the supported-employment initiative, developed the proposal package and coordinated inputs with internal teams.

What changed

The Thanksgiving campaign supported the acquisition of multiple large donations from New Jersey organizations. The employment initiative produced a researched prospect list and proposal package to advance external partnerships.

DVee’s Touch: Making a service more visible through a relatable story

Growth strategy, influencer campaign, video content | DVee’s Touch Massage Therapy

The challenge

The brand needed to reach beyond its existing audience and make its massage therapy services more discoverable to people looking for relevant local services.

What shaped my thinking

A straightforward service promotion would give people information, but a familiar and credible voice could help them see how the service fits into real life. I focused on connecting massage therapy to the pressures, routines and productivity of someone already making an impact in the community.

The strategy

Use an influencer-led story to make the service relatable, introduce it to new audiences and connect the content to the search language people use when looking for massage therapy in Ghana.

How I executed it

  • Developed and launched a campaign featuring a local social-impact leader, sharing her community work and how massage therapy helps her manage stress and stay productive.
  • Edited service and testimonial videos, using focused on-screen captions to make the content easier to follow and strengthen its message across TikTok and Instagram.

What changed

68.5% of campaign engagement and viewership came from new audiences. The content also improved discoverability around high-intent searches, including “massage therapist in Ghana.”
