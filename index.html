Expanding into Pennsylvania from zero presence

Email strategy, marketing automation, AI tooling | La Necar Logistics

The problem

The brand was expanding into Pennsylvania with no list, no reputation and no contacts. Buying a list wasn’t an option: disability-services outreach only works when the contact is relevant and reachable.

What I did

  • Used AI to build a Python contact-collection tool for a 9,000-contact disability-services directory, filtering by county, organization, active email, phone and website.
  • Onboarded 955 verified contacts to the CRM, then reused the tool to pass 1,000.
  • Designed and sent three targeted B2B campaigns a week, shaped by what families and providers were saying in Facebook groups and Reddit.
  • Tracked every enquiry that came in.

The result

Campaigns produced multiple qualified enquiries and transportation requests in a market with no prior presence. (Rider enquiries and requests aren’t shown here, as many contain private rider information.)

As the enquiries kept coming, I identified delays in fulfilling transportation requests. I led a team in reporting those delays, and their impact on brand reputation, to leadership.

Building a brand from inside the operation

Brand strategy, positioning, content | La Necar Logistics

The problem

Transportation companies tend to sound identical: reliable, affordable, on time. This one served adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities and the people who support them, and needed a voice that reflected that.

What I did

  • Worked with Operations, IT, Fleet, Customer Service and Behavior teams to build customer personas from real data and understand how rides actually run.
  • Turned those findings into the brand messaging, voice and identity now used across the company’s marketing, and wrote the website copy.
  • Proposed healthcare-provider outreach and prototyped “Ride Prescription Cards” so providers can direct patients to medical transport.
  • Ran an external survey of the disability community to lay the foundation for an advocacy marketing plan built on trust and referrals.

The result

A positioning grounded in the work itself, plus two new acquisition routes, healthcare providers and community advocacy, handed to the team ready to execute.

Turning marketing ideas into working AI tools

Product development, AI workflow design | Independent

The problem

Many marketing ideas die because the tool needed to test them doesn’t exist, and hiring developers for every experiment is slow.

What I built

  • Chat-to-Buy commerce system. Customers chat to get exactly what they need instead of browsing, designed to cut cart abandonment. Sold to an independent buyer.
  • AI virtual learning companion. A virtual teacher that walks learners through reports, papers and coursework, with a visual “TV” that breaks ideas down on screen. Sold to an independent buyer.
  • Lead-scraper web app. Built and deployed with Claude and VS Code: React, Python, Supabase and Netlify.

The result

Two sold products and a working method: describe the problem, build with AI, test with real users. I also run Ndu, a WhatsApp community that teaches AI-assisted web development and workflow automation.
